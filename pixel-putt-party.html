Game controls

  • Drag back and release to putt.
  • Only the player whose turn it is can shoot.
  • Balls collide with each other.
  • Complete 5 holes in each of 5 stages.

25 holes · 5 pixel-art stages

Play through five themed stages, each with five holes: Sunny Park, Sandy Dunes, Frostbyte Peaks, Lava Works and Neon Night. Each stage changes the course colours, obstacle layouts and challenge. Collect power-ups, bounce off bumpers and bump other players' balls!

Power-ups: Rocket adds extra shot strength, Bubble reduces friction for a longer roll, and Magnet pulls your ball gently toward the cup for one shot.
